How it was built

Data model, measures & workflow

Data model

  • Materials and Finished Goods
  • Suppliers, Purchase Orders and PO Lines
  • Production and Inventory Snapshot
  • Quality Checks and Dispatches
  • Calendar / date dimension

Build workflow

  • Imported and transformed structured data using Power Query.
  • Created relationships between operational and lookup tables.
  • Built DAX measures for manufacturing KPIs.
  • Designed interactive visuals, slicers and analytical views.

Selected measures

  • Production Achievement % and Rejection Rate %
  • Total Purchase Amount and Purchase Fulfilment Rate %
  • Inventory Value and Low Stock Items
  • Quality Pass Rate, Total Defects and Defect Rate %
  • On-Time Delivery %

Business questions explored

  • Is production meeting its planned target?
  • Which suppliers contribute most to procurement value?
  • Which items need replenishment?
  • How are quality defects trending?
  • How effectively are dispatches delivered on time?
